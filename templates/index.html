Operational Protocol

Verified End-to-End Activity Workflow

A transparent, role-governed process from event creation through report generation.

01

Event Sanctioning

Clubs publish events specifying category domain, schedule, participant capacity, and designated activity points for administrative review.

02

Student Registration

Students discover approved activities through the event catalog, verify seat quotas, and enroll directly through their dashboard.

03

Attendance Verification

Organizers verify student attendance on-site using dynamic session QR tokens or student roll number records.

04

Credit & Certification

Validated attendances immediately credit points to the student's tally, with downloadable participation certificates and PDF reports.
